The Role

What we're looking for

We're hiring part-time and full-time maths tutors for Primary 1 through Primary 6. Whether you're an MOE-trained teacher, a university student with a strong maths background, or an experienced tutor, we'd love to hear from you.

You'll be responsible for

  • Delivering engaging, MOE-aligned maths lessons in small group settings
  • Identifying and addressing individual learning gaps
  • Marking worksheets and providing constructive feedback
  • Communicating student progress to parents when needed
  • Contributing ideas to improve our teaching materials

You're a great fit if you

  • Have a genuine passion for maths and for teaching children
  • Are patient, encouraging, and clear in your explanations
  • Hold a degree in Education, Mathematics, or a related field — or have equivalent experience
  • Are familiar with the Singapore primary maths syllabus and PSLE format
  • Can commit to at least one consistent weekly slot

Nice to have

  • MOE teaching experience or prior tuition centre experience
  • Familiarity with the Singapore Model Method
  • Experience with P5 or P6 PSLE preparation

Quick answers

Do I need a teaching degree? +
Not necessarily. We welcome applicants with a degree in maths or a related field, strong subject knowledge, and a genuine ability to explain concepts clearly to children.
Is this part-time or full-time? +
Both are welcome. Many of our tutors work part-time alongside their studies or other commitments. We ask for at least one consistent weekly slot so students get continuity.
What is the pay? +
Competitive hourly rates, discussed during the interview. We review pay regularly and reward tutors who deliver strong results and receive positive parent feedback.
